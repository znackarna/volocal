### 2026-09-03 — Every comment out of the presentation page

His, in one line, after hearing the argument for keeping the seventy-one that
carry a finding: *dej okamžitě všechny komentáře pryč.* His page, his call, and
recorded here so nobody re-litigates it.

What went: 138 comments in the stylesheet, 19 in the two scripts, 21 in the
markup. 4 952 lines to 4 111; 306 KB to 243 KB.

**The reasoning is not lost, and that is why this is safe.** Everything those
comments carried is in this folder, which is where the project's rule says it
belongs — the two that were worth reading aloud are the Firefox `will-change`
finding in [2026-08-27](2026-08-27.md) and the tile seams that made
`backdrop-filter` give way to `filter` in [2026-08-26](2026-08-26.md). What the
page lost is a second copy beside the code; what nobody loses is the finding.

The risk this leaves is worth stating plainly: a comment beside a declaration
is read by whoever is about to change that declaration, and a day file is not.
So the two effects that get reintroduced by anybody who does not know — the
scale-and-promote blur, which is opposite in Chrome and Firefox, and the tiled
rasterisation — now depend on somebody reading this folder first.

**Nothing but comments changed, and that was proved rather than assumed.** With
comments and whitespace normalised away, the file before and the file after are
character-for-character identical. Both inline scripts parse. `translate.mjs
--check` is whole at 263, the same as before.

### 2026-09-03 — The title says what the program does, in his words

`<title>Volocal</title>` was seven characters and named only the brand, so
somebody searching for what the program does could not arrive here. The better
line existed already — `og:title` is *Zapněte počítač. Vypněte předplatné.* —
but that one is shown when a link is pasted, never when somebody is searching.

Three drafts were refused, and rightly: they were written for the search engine,
not for a reader — *Volocal — přepis nahrávek do textu ve vašem počítači* and
two more like it, category labels with a dash where this page speaks in
sentences. His own line replaced all of them:

> Volocal — přepis řeči bez cloudu a předplatného

47 characters, inside the 45–60 that survives uncut, and the name first because
whoever knows Volocal searches for it by name.

The description was 189 characters, and a result page cuts around 155 — so what
disappeared was *Bez ztráty kontroly a soukromí.*, the strongest sentence in it.
It was also a day out of date, still saying *rozpozná řečníky* and nothing about
languages. His replacement is 102 characters, so nothing is cut at all:

> Přepis řeči bez cloudu a předplatného. Běží ve vašem počítači, rozpozná
> jazyky, řečníky a opraví chyby.

He corrected it twice inside the minute, and both corrections are right. The
colon after *počítači* promised a definition and got a list, so it is a comma;
and *jazyk* became *jazyky*, because the singular claims only that the program
detects which language it is hearing, where the plural claims what shipped this
week — two of them in one recording, told apart.

*Bez cloudu* is the privacy claim said positively and said first, which is what
the old sentence could not manage from the far end of 189 characters.

English follows: *Volocal — speech to text without the cloud or a subscription*
(60) and *Speech to text without the cloud or a subscription. It runs on your
own computer: it spots the language, marks the speakers and fixes mistakes.*

**And a fault the English page turned up on the way.** `twitter:title` and
`twitter:description` were left out of the list in `translateTag` that decides
which `content` attributes are a machine quoting the page to a person — so
`/en/` had been serving a Czech Twitter card for as long as those tags have
existed. `og:` was in the list and `twitter:` was not, which is why nobody saw
it: the two carry the same words and only one of them was checked. Both are in
the list now, verified by reading the built English head rather than by
reasoning about the regex.

Files: `docs/site/index.html`, `docs/site/en.json`, `docs/site/translate.mjs`.

### 2026-09-03 — The description stops repeating the title

He noticed it in the English and it was true of both: the title said *přepis
řeči bez cloudu a předplatného* and the description opened with the same words.
In a search result those two lines sit one above the other, so half the
description was spent saying again what the reader had just read.

His replacement says what the title does not:

> Rozpozná jazyky, řečníky a opraví chyby. Zvuk, video i online stream.
> Zdarma pro Windows 10 a 11.

96 characters, so nothing is cut, and it took three passes to get there because
the first two were still repeating the title. He caught it himself: *bez cloudu
je jasné, že to je lokálně, ne?* It was doubled twice over — the title's *bez
cloudu* and the description's *běží pouze ve vašem počítači* are one claim, and
so are *bez předplatného* and *zdarma*. What is left is only what the title does
not say: two languages in one recording, the speakers, the corrections, and that
it takes video and streams as well as sound.

The English drops the subject the same way, verb first, which is the register
the rest of the page's short claims are already in: *Spots the languages, marks
the speakers and fixes mistakes. Audio, video and online streams. Free for
Windows 10 and 11.*

**What this costs, recorded so the decision is not re-made by accident.** In a
search result the title sits directly above the description, so the repetition
was pure waste. In a pasted link it does not: there the line above is *Zapněte
počítač. Vypněte předplatné.*, which says nothing about the cloud — so the card
in Slack or on LinkedIn no longer mentions privacy at all. The two could be
split, `description` for search and `og:description` for the card, at the price
of one more text to keep in step. Not done, and his to reverse.

English follows: *Volocal runs only on your own computer. It spots the
languages, marks the speakers and fixes mistakes. Free, for Windows 10 and 11.*

Files: `docs/site/index.html`, `docs/site/en.json`.
