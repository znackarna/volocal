# 2026-08-21

### 2026-08-21 — The gains section on a phone, where it had nothing to show

The ten rows under *Co získáte?* carry a card of the application's own markup,
built on 20 August. Below 900 px it read:

```css
@media (max-width: 900px) { .row-reveal { display: none; } }
```

So every phone and every tablet held upright got the sentences and no proof at
all — the argument the section is built on, gone at the width where most of a
link's traffic reads it. Three more faults came out of measuring the same
widths, and one of them made the page scroll sideways.

#### The card comes into the flow rather than being taken away

The reason it was an overlay is that a card which unfolds moves everything
under it, and that reason is a two-column reason. In one column the row has the
width to itself and there is nothing to push. So below 900 px the fragment is
`position: static`, in `grid-column: 2` beside the icon, 92 px tall, with no
transition and nothing hidden: the proof stands under the sentence that makes
the claim.

The room turned out to be there already. The fragments were drawn for the
526 px column of the two-column grid, and one column is **820 px at 899 and
565 px at 620** — wider than what they were built in, all the way down to the
phone breakpoint.

Two fragments needed the width taken seriously:

- **The header bar** carries the mark, three marks for where sound comes from,
  and the running recorder pill. At 299 px they overlapped. The mark goes below
  620 px — the page wears it in its own header two screens up, and the card is
  about the other two — and the pill takes what is left instead of its fixed
  290 px. The application folds that pill at exactly 900 px, which is right in a
  380 px window and wrong in a picture of a 1280 px one, so the fragment keeps
  its waves and its name.
- **The eight format sheets** are 465 px of fixed widths. They are a share of
  the card now, capped at the 52 px they are drawn at.

#### `min-height: 128px` outlived the card it was for

Every row that can open has a floor of 128 px so the card clears the hairline
evenly. That rule was never turned off with the card, so below 900 px each row
kept 48 px of empty space under text that measures 79. Ten rows, 480 px of
nothing, and the section stood at 2016 px where its writing needs about 1540.

#### The loop cards never started

Three cards animate — the corrected word, the three file names arriving, the
subtitle sweep — and the loop is started by `pointerenter` or `focusin`, since
until now the card was only ever seen while hovered. On a phone neither ever
fires, so *Sledovaná složka* stood empty: its file rows begin at `opacity: 0`
and nothing was there to bring them in.

Below 900 px an `IntersectionObserver` starts each one as it arrives on screen,
once and then never again — a picture that replays every time it scrolls past is
a flicker rather than a proof. The wordmark in the header bar is drawn the same
way; the two paths share one `startRow`, so hover and scroll cannot both draw it.

#### The page scrolled sideways, and the number was in the stylesheet

`.carousel-viewport` takes 48 px of horizontal padding for the window's shadow
and gives it straight back as `margin: -48px`, which puts the box 48 px past its
column on each side. The page's gutter is 32. So **every window narrower than
1276 px scrolled sideways by exactly 16 px**, and below 620 px, where the gutter
is 20, by 28.

The amount is the gutter now, at both breakpoints. It is not a number of its
own and never was — the negative margin makes it the overhang, and the overhang
has to be something the page can afford.

At 320 px there was a second one: the mark and the three places to go want
346 px of a 305 px line. They wrap now, rather than one of them being taken
away.

#### Nineteen tab stops behind an invisible card

The ten cards hold 19 buttons of the application's own furniture — a time mark,
a rename, a stop. `.row-reveal` is `opacity: 0` and `pointer-events: none` when
shut, neither of which takes anything out of the tab order, so a keyboard was
walking through window furniture nobody could see on the way to *Stáhnout*.
Every card is `inert`.

What it costs: `:focus-within` was the keyboard's way of opening a card, and
with the contents inert nothing inside can hold focus, so on a desktop the
cards are now a pointer affordance only. That is the right trade — the sentence
carries the claim and the card illustrates it — and below 900 px the question
does not arise, because there the card is simply open.

#### Measured, at fifteen widths

`document.scrollWidth` against `clientWidth` from 320 to 1440 px: **no sideways
scroll at any width**, where before there was 16 px from 621 to 1276, 28 px
below 620 and 41 px at 320. No card clips its own frame at any width except
320 px, where two cut a hairline (10 px and 2 px) inside their own 92 px face.

The desktop is untouched by all of it: the section measures 1131 px at 1280 and
1440 px, the same as before the change, and the cards still arrive on hover.

Files: `docs/site/index.html` — the stylesheet's 900 px and 620 px blocks, a new
420 px block, `.carousel-viewport`, `.save-formats`/`.fmt`, `inert` on the ten
cards, and the boot script's row-staging.
