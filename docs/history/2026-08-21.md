# 2026-08-21

### 2026-08-21 — Documents written for people, and a rule that says so

> Chci aby všechny dokumenty byly napsané pro lidi. Ne jen hrubé překlady, ale
> aby anglická i česká verze byly lidsky čitelné a bez zpráv pro modely, kalků
> a dalších trapáren.

Yesterday's `SECURITY.md` was cut from 2934 words to 1723 and still opened with
this:

> Česky i anglicky. Uživatelé jsou čeští, ale ten, kdo najde chybu, nemusí být.

A note explaining how the document is built, to a reader who can see it is in
two languages. Four lines further down came the sentence that ended the
argument:

> **Nic si z internetu nenačítá.** Obrazovky, které vidíte, jsou součástí
> nainstalovaného programu — nejsou to stránky, které by se odněkud stahovaly.
> Nemá tedy kudy dovnitř pustit cizí stránku, reklamu, sledovací skript ani nic
> jiného, co by někdo vydával za Volocal.

Three sentences saying one thing, and the last clause meaning nothing — nobody
is pretending to be Volocal. His word for it was slop, and it was.

It is one line now:

> **Rozhraní není webová stránka.** Je součástí programu, takže se do něj nemá
> jak dostat reklama ani sledování.

#### What came out

Seven cuts, each the same kind of sentence — one that serves the text rather
than the reader:

- *Slibovat konkrétní lhůtu by znamenalo slibovat něco, co nikdo nedodrží.* —
  explains the sentence above it, which already said a reply can take days.
- *Podporovaná je vždycky ta poslední.* — the sentence before it, again.
- *…a je to popsané hned níž* — the heading is directly below.
- *Co to nezastaví, **a je poctivé to říct**…* — praising the document's own
  honesty inside the document.
- *Má to ale následek, **který musíte vědět**…* — filler in front of the
  consequence.
- *Ještě tři drobnosti, které…* — counting the items instead of saying them.
- *Nic neodesílá. Nahrávky, přepisy ani nastavení počítač neopouštějí.* — one
  message twice.

And three phrasings: *Kam to hlásit* → *Kam hlásit chybu*, *prohlížecí okno* →
*okno*, and a verbless fragment (*A přístup k souborům.*) folded into a sentence.

#### The rule

`CLAUDE.md` gained a section, because this is the third time the same fault has
been repaired by hand.

**Say each thing once. Czech written as Czech, with no calques. Both halves of
a bilingual document identical in content — heading for heading, point for
point — and each written as if a person wrote it in that language. Lead with
what the reader gets, not with the mechanism.**

`docs-check.mjs` already counts headings and numbered points on each side, so
the parity half of the rule is enforced rather than trusted. The two halves now
carry thirteen matching elements in the same order.

**Nothing addressed to a model belongs in a document a person reads.** That is
the sentence the whole day turned on, and it is worth keeping separate from the
rest: `CLAUDE.md` talks to models, everything else talks to people.
