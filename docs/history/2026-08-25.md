### 2026-08-25 — The first FAQ answer is the owner's again, and it brings a claim nothing here measures

His replacement for the answer under *Sedí časy slov přesně?*:

> Pro sledování textu jsou dostatečné, ale u dlouhé věty s pauzou uprostřed se
> mohou mírně posunout. Časové značky jsou přesnější u robustnějšího modelu.

It is in. Two things in it are worth writing down.

**A number went out.** *Asi o vteřinu* became *mírně*. The second is safer and
the first was the more useful sentence — a reader can picture a second and
cannot picture *mírně*. His copy, his call, and it is recorded here rather than
argued.

**A new fact arrived, and this repository does not hold it.** *Časové značky
jsou přesnější u robustnějšího modelu* is a claim about the product that
nothing here has measured:

- `dtw_preset` in `transcription/whisper.rs` hands **every** model an alignment
  preset — `large.v3`, `medium`, `small`, `base`. Both offered models get DTW
  word timestamps; neither is running without alignment.
- The only alignment ever measured here is large-v3's, in `CLAUDE.md`: 881
  words across five windows, median offset −0.08 to +0.03 s. **There is no
  fast-model run to compare it against.** The measurement proves large-v3 is
  accurate, not that it is *more* accurate than the other.

The claim is very likely true — a bigger Whisper model aligns better, and he
uses this program every day. But `docs/site/README.md` carries a table of every
fact the page asserts and where it came from, and the rule beside it is that a
new claim adds its source. So the row is there, and what it says is that the
source is his judgement from use and not a measurement in this repository.

That is the same rule that keeps recommended hardware off the page: figures
would have to be measured before they could be written. This one is written
because the owner wrote it; the table now says so out loud, which is the least
that honesty costs. Two runs of the same audio, one per model, would settle it
and would be worth the hour.
