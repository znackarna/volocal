### 2026-08-25 — The first FAQ answer is the owner's again, and it brings a claim nothing here measures

His replacement for the answer under *Sedí časy slov přesně?*:

> Pro sledování textu jsou dostatečné, ale u dlouhé věty s pauzou uprostřed se
> mohou mírně posunout. Časové značky jsou přesnější u robustnějšího modelu.

It is in. Two things in it are worth writing down.

**A number went out.** *Asi o vteřinu* became *mírně*. The second is safer and
the first was the more useful sentence — a reader can picture a second and
cannot picture *mírně*. His copy, his call, and it is recorded here rather than
argued.

**A new fact arrived, and this repository does not hold it.** *Časové značky
jsou přesnější u robustnějšího modelu* is a claim about the product that
nothing here has measured:

- `dtw_preset` in `transcription/whisper.rs` hands **every** model an alignment
  preset — `large.v3`, `medium`, `small`, `base`. Both offered models get DTW
  word timestamps; neither is running without alignment.
- The only alignment ever measured here is large-v3's, in `CLAUDE.md`: 881
  words across five windows, median offset −0.08 to +0.03 s. **There is no
  fast-model run to compare it against.** The measurement proves large-v3 is
  accurate, not that it is *more* accurate than the other.

The claim is very likely true — a bigger Whisper model aligns better, and he
uses this program every day. But `docs/site/README.md` carries a table of every
fact the page asserts and where it came from, and the rule beside it is that a
new claim adds its source. So the row is there, and what it says is that the
source is his judgement from use and not a measurement in this repository.

That is the same rule that keeps recommended hardware off the page: figures
would have to be measured before they could be written. This one is written
because the owner wrote it; the table now says so out loud, which is the least
that honesty costs. Two runs of the same audio, one per model, would settle it
and would be worth the hour.

### 2026-08-25 — The second FAQ answer is his, and it disagrees with the third

His replacement for the answer under *Co když mluví dva přes sebe?*:

> Volocal bez problémů rozliší jednotlivé mluvčí, ale ve skupině hlasů si
> vybere ten hlasitější. V nepřehledné situaci vám pomůže ruční oprava.

In, because it is his page and his voice. Two things are wrong with it and both
are worth having written down rather than quietly fixed.

**It contradicts the answer two rows below it.** Side by side, as they now
stand in the same section:

| | |
|---|---|
| second answer | *Volocal **bez problémů** rozliší jednotlivé mluvčí* |
| third answer | *Pět lidí v jedné místnosti **sloučilo jednu dvojici*** |

Both cannot be true. The third is a measurement of something that happened; the
second says it does not happen. A section whose whole job is to admit limits
now overclaims in its second sentence and takes it back in its third, and a
reader who notices trusts neither.

**It does not answer the question it is under.** The question is *Co když mluví
dva přes sebe* — overlapping speech. The new answer opens on speaker separation
in general, which is a different thing, and only reaches the overlap in its
second clause. The sentence it replaced started with *Řeč přes sebe se slije*
and was answering from its first word.

The smallest repair is two words: **drop *bez problémů***. *Volocal rozliší
jednotlivé mluvčí, ale ve skupině hlasů si vybere ten hlasitější* keeps his
sentence, his rhythm and his order, and stops disagreeing with the row below.
Offered to him; not applied, because rewriting an owner's copy on a judgement
call is how a page stops sounding like the person who wrote it.

### 2026-08-25 — The FAQ's lede, and what it stopped doing

His line under *Jaké má limity?*:

> Volocal je mocný pomocník, ale má svá omezení.

It replaces *Radši si to přečtěte teď, než na to přijdete později.*

In, as written. What changed is not the wording but the job the sentence does.
The old one spoke to the reader and gave them a reason to open the rows: read
this now, or meet it later. The new one describes the product, and the half of
it that describes it is praise — *mocný pomocník* — standing at the top of the
one section on the page whose entire purpose is to admit what the program
cannot do. A page that opens its limits by complimenting itself is read as
sales, and the rows underneath then have to win the reader back.

Recorded because the owner has an opinion about exactly this register already:
on 19 August his verdict on an arrangement of this page was *vypadá to jako
každý jiný AI cringe*, and he was right about the genre. *Mocný pomocník* is
that genre in three words.

The version that keeps his sentence and loses the sales, offered rather than
applied:

> Volocal má svá omezení. Radši si o nich přečtěte teď, než na ně přijdete
> později.

His opening clause, his structure, the old sentence's job restored to it.

This is the third of his lines today that went in with a note beside it, and
the notes are getting shorter on purpose: the page is his voice and the record
exists so that a decision can be revisited, not so that it can be argued twice.

### 2026-08-25 — The third answer, and the pattern under all four

His replacement under *Zvládne to poradu s pěti lidmi?*:

> Strukturované rozhovory ve více lidech ano, ale chaotické panelové diskuse
> spíš ne. Dva hlasy většinou sedí. Ale více lidí, kteří si skáčou do řeči,
> jsou zatím výzva.

In, with **one comma added** — the clause `kteří si skáčou do řeči` was left
open in the comment and its absence is a typo rather than a choice. Worth
noting that *více lidí … jsou* takes a singular verb in careful Czech; that one
is left alone, being a matter of ear rather than of rule.

**The contradiction flagged an hour ago has gone, and by the wrong door.** The
second answer still says Volocal tells speakers apart *bez problémů*; what it
disagreed with — *Pět lidí v jedné místnosti sloučilo jednu dvojici* — is no
longer on the page. The two sentences no longer clash because the measured one
left, not because the claim did. That is worth having written down: a
disagreement resolved by deleting the evidence looks, in a diff, exactly like
one that was fixed.

#### The pattern, said once

Four edits to this section today, and every one moves the same direction:

| was | is |
|---|---|
| Radši si to přečtěte teď, než na to přijdete později | Volocal je mocný pomocník, ale má svá omezení |
| se můžou rozejít **asi o vteřinu** | se mohou **mírně** posunout |
| Řeč přes sebe se slije | Volocal **bez problémů** rozliší jednotlivé mluvčí |
| **Pět lidí v jedné místnosti sloučilo jednu dvojici** | více lidí … **jsou zatím výzva** |

Every specific left; every replacement is a degree word. The section began as
five things somebody had watched happen and is now five things somebody feels
about the program. That is not a copy preference — it is what the section is
for. A limits page earns trust by naming an amount a reader can picture: a
second, five people, one pair merged. *Mírně* and *zatím výzva* cost nothing to
say and are worth what they cost.

Said once, here, and not again. The page is the owner's voice and this record
exists so a decision can be revisited, not argued twice. If he wants the
numbers back, the sentences they came from are three entries above this one and
in the 18 August comment they were parked in.

### 2026-08-25 — The tab wore the cube, and a pasted link wore a retired joke

Two things the owner spotted on the published page, both in `<head>` and
neither visible from inside the page itself.

#### The favicon was the mark that left on 16 August

The tab showed three coloured rhombi and a circle — the Značkárna cube, which
this project stopped using on 16 August (*the cube leaves and the name closes
to its own middle*). It had been sitting in a `data:` URI in the head since
before that, where nothing that renames a mark would ever look.

It is the application's own icon now, `src-tauri/icons/app-icon.svg` — the
white squircle with *olo* and its smile, the same thing Windows shows for the
installed program. Kept as a `data:` URI rather than a file on purpose:
`bundle.py` inlines stylesheets, scripts and fonts but not a linked icon, so a
file would leave the single-file copy with no icon at all. 2 990 bytes.

Checked at 16, 32, 64 and 128 px: the smile survives the smallest size, which
is the one that matters.

#### The description was still selling the headline that was replaced

A link pasted into a chat window showed:

> Co spolu přepíšeme? Zahoď notes, zapni noťas. Volocal převádí mluvené slovo
> na text přímo ve vašem počítači — zvukový soubor, mikrofon i odkaz na
> YouTube.

Three things wrong with it, all invisible on the page. *Zahoď notes, zapni
noťas* was the informal headline retired on 19 August when the page went to
vykání — so the one sentence most likely to be read first was the only place
still tykající. And *odkaz na YouTube* names one service where the page names
online import generally; SoundCloud works too, and Spotify deliberately does
not.

The new one is the page's own current copy, recombined rather than invented:

> Zapněte počítač, vypněte předplatné. Volocal přepisuje mluvené slovo přímo ve
> vašem počítači — zvukový soubor, mikrofon i odkaz na online službu. Rozpozná
> řečníky a nic neodesílá.

#### And nothing told a chat window what the page is

There were no Open Graph tags at all, which is why the preview was three lines
of grey text and a domain: with nothing to go on, a chat window falls back to
`<title>` and the description. `og:type`, `og:url`, `og:site_name`,
`og:locale`, `og:title`, `og:description` and a Twitter summary card are in.

**`og:image` is deliberately absent.** There is no picture of this product that
is not a screenshot of a window, and a preview card carrying a bad image is
worse than one carrying none — the card shrinks the text to make room for it.
A real 1200 × 630 image is worth making and is the owner's to approve; the
comment in the head says why it is not there, so nobody adds a hasty one.
