### 2026-08-25 — *Co nedělá dobře* becomes *Jaké má limity?*

The owner, from a comment anchored to that heading. The section is a FAQ, so a
question at the top of it is the right shape — every heading under it is one,
and the old one was the only statement in the block.

What it costs, said here because it was worth saying at the time: *Co nedělá
dobře* admitted a fault in the program's own voice, and a page that says that
about itself buys trust with it. *Jaké má limity?* asks a neutral question and
lets the answers do the admitting. The answers are unchanged and still his, so
nothing is actually softened — only the sign over them.

The lede under it, *Radši si to přečtěte teď, než na to přijdete později*, reads
the same either way and stayed.

`docs/site/README.md` names the heading in its handover list and was corrected
with it. Nothing links to the section by text; `#faq` is the id and it did not
move.
