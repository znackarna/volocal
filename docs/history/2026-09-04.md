# 2026-09-04

### 2026-09-04 — Two more cards in Co získáte?, for the two features that shipped

The page had ten small cards under the three large ones and said nothing about
either feature that landed this week. It has twelve now.

**Druhý jazyk nezůstane stranou.** His copy: *Pokud nahrávka obsahuje druhý
jazyk, Volocal si toho všimne a přepis doplní. Ideální pro záznamy s živým
překladatelem.* The second sentence is the one that sells it — it names the
room the feature was built for, which nothing in my drafts did.

The snippet is `SecondLanguageBar` as the program draws it: the notice ground,
the transcription line-icon, *V nahrávce zní také angličtina. Chcete doplnit
přepis?* and the two buttons. At his ask the blue fills the whole 490 × 92 face
rather than floating in the middle of it, which is also how the bar behaves in
the application — it runs the width of the content.

**Vystřihnete jen ten kus.** A passage marked in the transcript with the
context menu open on *Exportovat výběr*. The gesture is the feature: there is no
mode and nothing to learn, you drag over the words you want and right-click.
Showing the save dialog instead would have made a second picture of format
cards, which the card above it already is.

**Two page-side classes, and they are not the application's.** `.word.picked`
paints a selection the browser makes for real and cannot be captured in static
markup, and `.clip-pair` lays the menu beside the text. Both live in
`page.css`, not in `app-shot.css`, which is generated and must not be
hand-edited.

**The bug worth recording.** Twice I called the two blocks the same size on the
strength of a measurement at 1280 px, where every face is 490 × 92, and twice he
said they were not. Below 700 px the reveal is shown statically and its height
follows its content: the other ten fit inside 92, mine did not — 105 for the
language bar, 156 for the clip, and 217 at 420 px. A measurement at one width
is not a measurement of a responsive page. Fixed by shortening the quoted
sentence, letting the menu shrink, and dropping its second item below 700 px;
then measured at 420, 560, 700, 900 and 1280, where all twelve are 92.

Files: `docs/site/index.html`, `docs/site/page.css`, `docs/site/en.json`.

### 2026-09-04 — The clip menu takes the padding the other snippets use

His, from a comment: the menu sat too close to the edge of its block.

It had 16 px, which was a number I invented. The snippets next to it do not:
`.row-face.watch` is 20 and `.row-face.cue` is 22, so the menu is 22 now and the
page has one fewer value in it than it would have had.

Below 700 px it stays 16, and that is measured rather than preferred: at 22 the
quoted sentence takes one more line and the block grows from 92 to 96 — the very
unevenness he had caught an hour earlier. Checked at 380, 420, 560, 700, 900 and
1280, where all twelve blocks are 92.
