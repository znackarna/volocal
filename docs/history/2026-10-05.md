# 2026-10-05

### 2026-10-05 — Two things the screenshots showed: `vychozi` on screen, and a help that lost its last rows

Pictures of `volocal-tui` were made for the owner to show, drawn from the
program's own test terminal with its colours. Two faults showed in them.

**`vychozi` was printed as it is stored.** `tools::choose_compute` answers
`vychozi` when whisper has no build of its own for a device and chooses where
it computes itself, and also when no whisper is found. The window never shows
that value as text; the terminal showed it raw, on the Status screen (*Počítá
na vychozi*) and under a running transcription's bar. It is now *zařízení,
které zvolí whisper* on Status and *zařízení volí whisper* under the bar,
*device whisper chooses* in English.

**The help lost two rows when playback was offered.** The two playback rows of
4 October pushed *Ctrl+C* and *q* out of the bottom of the dialog, and *Přehrát
od úseku, pozastavit* was cut off at its column's edge. The dialog is two rows
taller when the playback keys are listed, and the line is *Přehrát,
pozastavit* (*Play, pause*).

Verified: the help test now asserts both playback rows and the last row of the
last group; a test of the three new words; clippy with `-D warnings` and the 38
tests of `volocal-tui` under Wine; the redrawn help in the pictures.

Files: `src-tauri/src/bin/volocal-tui/{words,app}.rs`.
