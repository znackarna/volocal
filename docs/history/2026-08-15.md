# 2026-08-15

### 2026-08-15 — The empty dictionary is a row to write in, not a sentence saying it is empty

> Pokud je slovník prázdný ukáže se tam text `Slovník je zatím prázdný.` Já bych
> tam raději nechal jeden prázdný řádek tabulky připravené pro zápis.

The card had two states and only one of them was a table. With entries it was
the list the day before had spent itself getting right — two marks, two fields,
a bin, 56 px. With none it was a line of grey prose inside the same panel,
`Slovník je zatím prázdný.`, and a button underneath.

**The sentence described the table instead of offering it.** It said the one
thing a reader can already see — there is nothing here — and said nothing about
what a row of this table is or that they may write one. Everything that would
answer that was in the shape the empty state replaced: the red cross in front of
the wrong word, the green tick in front of the right one, and the two
placeholders that show a real pair. So the first thing a new installation showed
of its dictionary was the one drawing of it that teaches nothing.

A row standing ready costs the same band of screen, needs no reading, and takes
the first keystroke.

#### The standing row is the draft row, before anybody has typed

Yesterday's rebuild is what makes this small. The unwritten row already lives in
the interface rather than in the archive, drawn by the same block as the saved
ones and told apart by `DRAFT_ROW`. An empty table now appends that row whether
or not a draft exists:

```
const dictionaryRows = draft || dictionary.length === 0
  ? [...dictionary, { id: DRAFT_ROW, find: draft?.find ?? "", replace: draft?.replace ?? "" }]
  : dictionary;
```

`draft` keeps its meaning — *somebody is writing this* — and the row no longer
depends on it. That separation is what the two following decisions are argued
from, and it is why `dictionaryRows` can never be empty: the branch that drew
the sentence is gone rather than left unreachable.

Typing is what turns the standing row into a draft, so both fields start one
instead of requiring one (`current ?? EMPTY_DRAFT`). Leaving it is unchanged and
needs no case of its own: `closeDraft` returns early with no draft, and
`addEntry` has always refused a half-filled pair, so an abandoned row goes back
to being the empty one.

#### Two things the row must not do

**It must not take the cursor.** The callback ref that focuses the first field
is what makes `Přidat výraz` good — press it and you are typing. A row that is
there because a table happens to be empty has asked for nothing, and focusing it
would move the cursor out of whatever the reader opened Settings for, on a tab
that also holds the model cards and the language. So the ref is `writing &&
draft`: it attaches on the first keystroke, where `focus()` lands on the field
already being typed into and does nothing. Pressing the button on an empty table
still focuses the row — the button now means *put me in it* rather than *make
one*, which is the same promise from the reader's side.

**It must not offer a bin.** The standing row cannot be thrown away; the table
would draw it again. Yesterday's listing work was about exactly this — a control
must not claim something it cannot do — so the bin is not drawn while nothing
has been typed. The first character makes the row a draft and the bin appears
with its ordinary meaning: what you typed goes. Its 26 px column is declared in
`grid-template-columns`, so the empty cell moves nothing and the row keeps the
listing's right edge.

- Files: `src/Settings.tsx`, `src/locales/cs/settings.ts`,
  `src/locales/en/settings.ts`, `src/locales/sources.json`.
- `settings.dictionary.empty` is deleted in both languages and in the source
  notes. No screen can reach it now, and a key nothing renders is copy that
  reads as current when it is not.
- `settings.dictionary.saving` is untouched and still true: changes save
  themselves, an unfinished row is discarded.
- Verified: `npm run build` — `i18n:check`, `docs:check`, `tsc --noEmit` and the
  Vite build all clean; `npm test` 132.
- Not verified by machine: delete the last entry and the table must become one
  empty row with both placeholders and no bin, with the cursor left where it
  was. Type one character and the bin must appear. Leave the row half-filled and
  it must empty rather than save. Open Settings on `Přepis` with an empty
  dictionary and nothing may steal the focus.

### 2026-08-15 — The dictionary's placeholders say what goes in the field, not what it might look like

> Místo `součas DNA` tam dejme spíš placeholder který vysvětlí, že se tam má
> napsat špatný výraz a dobrý výraz.

The entry above put a standing row in an empty table and argued that its two
placeholders are what teach a reader what a row is. They were not doing that.
They held `součas DNA` and `součást DNA` — a real mishearing and its repair,
which is a good *example* and not an *explanation*. A reader meeting an empty
dictionary saw two half-words and had to work backwards from them to the rule.

**A placeholder is the field's own instruction when nothing else on screen is.**
Both names exist — `Co přepis slyší` and `Jak to má být` — but they are the
`aria-label` and the marks' hover title, so on a screen being looked at they say
nothing. The two boxes were the only visible words, and they spent them on a
sample.

So: `Špatný výraz` and `Správný výraz`.

- **`Správný`, not `dobrý`** as asked. A word is not good or bad in itself, it
  is right or wrong in this transcript — and `správný` is what the green tick in
  front of the field already means.
- **`Výraz`** for the reason `Přidat výraz` carries it: the pair may be a phrase
  rather than a word, and `záznam` is a recording everywhere else here.
- Capital first letter and no full stop, which is `settings.files.watchPlaceholder`'s
  shape — the one other placeholder in Settings that names a state rather than
  showing a sample.

**The example was also a translation problem**, which is the part worth keeping.
A mishearing is language-specific: `součas DNA` cannot be translated, only
re-invented, and every future language would need somebody to find a plausible
error in it. The English pair had already drifted into `part of dee-en-ay`,
which is a phonetic joke rather than a transcription mistake. An instruction
translates.

- Files: `src/locales/cs/settings.ts`, `src/locales/en/settings.ts`,
  `src/locales/sources.json`.
- English is `Wrong term` / `Right term`, and both keys were re-approved with
  `i18n.mjs approve en` so the fingerprints match today's Czech.
- Verified: `npm run build` — `i18n:check` clean, with the same 17 untranslated
  keys and the same single inconsistency as before the change; `npm test` 132.
- Not verified by machine: with an empty dictionary the standing row must read
  `Špatný výraz` / `Správný výraz`, and the placeholders must still be absent
  from saved rows.

### 2026-08-15 — `Chybný`, not `Špatný`

> Dejme `Chybný` místo `špatný`.

The entry above chose `Špatný výraz` and argued the pairing from the right-hand
field: `správný` and not `dobrý`, because a word is not good or bad in itself.
The argument was right and it was applied to only one of the two fields.

`Špatný` is the same fault on the left. A *špatný výraz* is one badly chosen —
a judgement about the wording. What the dictionary holds is not that: it is what
the transcript **misheard**, and `chybný` says exactly that and nothing more.

It also makes the row one pair instead of two halves from different pairs.
`Chybný` ↔ `Správný` is a single opposition in Czech; `špatný` pulls towards
`dobrý`, which the field beside it had already refused. Two fields sitting
12 px apart must not each be reaching for a different antonym.

- Files: `src/locales/cs/settings.ts`, `src/locales/sources.json`.
- **English is unchanged.** `Wrong term` was already the precise word — English
  `wrong` is the antonym of `right` and carries none of `špatný`'s *bad*. Only
  the fingerprint was refreshed, so the pair is recorded as matching today's
  Czech rather than left reading as stale.
- Verified: `npm run build`; `npm test` 132.

### 2026-08-15 — The model note names the reader's act, not the model's condition

> Chybějící model se výběrem automaticky stáhne.
> Výběr nestaženého modelu zahájí stahování.

The sentence under the model cards, rewritten. Its job did not change — the
translator note calls it a **promise about behaviour** and warns against turning
it back into a description of what the list contains — but it was making that
promise about the wrong subject.

**`Chybějící model` is a property of a model; `výběr` is something the reader
does.** A note under a row of cards is read by somebody deciding whether to
press one, and the old sentence answered a question about the catalogue on the
way to answering theirs. The new one starts where they are.

Two words went with it:

- **`automaticky` carried nothing.** `se stáhne` is already the thing happening
  by itself; the adverb was the sentence reassuring itself.
- **`chybějící` → `nestažený`.** A model that has never been fetched is not
  missing — nothing is wrong and nothing is absent that should be there. It is
  simply not here yet, which is what `nestažený` says and what the card beside
  it shows by printing a size instead of a tick.

The repetition `nestaženého` … `stahování` is real and is the price of naming
both the state and the act with the one verb this application uses for both.
Reaching for a synonym to avoid it would put a second word for downloading on a
screen that has spent the week getting to one.

- Files: `src/locales/cs/settings.ts`, `src/locales/en/settings.ts`,
  `src/locales/sources.json`.
- English follows the same move: `Picking a model you have not downloaded
  starts the download.` — the act first, where it had been `A model you do not
  have is downloaded when you pick it.`
- The translator note now records the old sentence and why it went, so the next
  rewrite does not walk back into the subject that was just taken out.
- Verified: `npm run build` — `i18n:check` clean with the same 17 untranslated
  keys and the same single inconsistency as before; `npm test` 132.
- Not verified by machine: the sentence is an `InfoNote` under the model cards
  on `Přepis`, and it should still be one line at the settings column's width.
