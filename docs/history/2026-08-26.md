# 2026-08-26

### 2026-08-26 — The cube comes back out of the panel

His call, an hour after it went in and two hours after he drew it: the mark is
out and the panel is a sentence and a button again.

Both the group around them and the rule that sized the drawing went with it, so
the panel is the markup it was before — nothing left standing that has nothing
to hold. What stays written down is why it was worth trying: the panel is the
one place on the page that is not about the program, which is what let a piece
of full colour stand there at all.

The three ways it could have assembled itself are not in the repository. They
were built as a page to choose from, the shapes growing from their own centres
at his ask, and no letter was chosen before the cube was withdrawn.
